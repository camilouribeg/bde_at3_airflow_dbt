\section{Business Questions Analysis (Part 4)}

\subsection{Overview}
Part 4 analyzes key business questions using SQL queries on the data warehouse. Results provide insights into demographic correlations, revenue patterns, and host performance across Sydney LGAs. SQL queries are in the Appendix (SS-1 to SS-5).

\subsection{Business Questions}

\subsubsection{Question 1: Demographic Differences Analysis}
\textbf{Question:} What are the demographic differences between the top 3 performing and lowest 3 performing LGAs based on estimated revenue per active listing over the available 12 months?

\textbf{Analysis:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Revenue Performance:} The top 3 LGAs (Mosman \$12,162, Hunters Hill \$10,231, Woollahra \$10,007) significantly outperform the bottom 3 (Burwood \$3,237, Hornsby \$2,535, Liverpool \$2,175).
\item \textbf{Demographic Distribution:} Gender distribution shows minimal variation across LGAs, with most areas maintaining balanced male/female ratios around 48-52\%.
\item \textbf{Age Correlation:} Top-performing LGAs have higher median ages (Mosman 43, Hunters Hill 39, Woollahra 41) compared to bottom performers (Burwood 33, Liverpool 33, Hornsby 40), suggesting mature, affluent demographics drive higher revenue.
\item \textbf{Population Size:} Smaller LGAs generate higher revenue per listing - top performers have populations of 28,000-40,000, while bottom performers range from 200,000-400,000 residents, indicating scale advantages for smaller, affluent areas.
\end{itemize}

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.9\textwidth]{images/q1_complete_demographic_analysis.png}
  \caption{Complete demographic analysis showing revenue, gender distribution, age correlation, and population size for top 3 vs bottom 3 LGAs.}
\end{figure}

\textbf{Key Finding:} Top-performing LGAs are smaller, more affluent areas with higher property values, while bottom performers are larger, more diverse regions.

\subsubsection{Question 2: Age-Revenue Correlation}
\textbf{Question:} Is there a correlation between the median age of a neighbourhood and the revenue generated per active listing in that neighbourhood?

\textbf{Analysis:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Correlation Analysis:} Strong positive correlation (r = 0.6815) between median age and revenue. Older neighborhoods generate higher revenue, explaining 46.4\% of revenue variation.
\end{itemize}

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.9\textwidth]{images/q2_age_revenue_correlation.png}
  \caption{Age vs revenue correlation analysis showing strong positive relationship.}
\end{figure}

\textbf{Key Finding:} Age demographics significantly influence Airbnb performance, with mature areas commanding premium rates.

\subsubsection{Question 3: Optimal Property Types}
\textbf{Question:} What is the best type of listing for the top 5 neighbourhoods to achieve the highest number of stays?

\textbf{Analysis:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Top Neighbourhoods:} Mosman leads with \$12,162 revenue and 1,179 stays, followed by Sutherland Shire (\$11,835, 780 stays).
\item \textbf{Best Property Type:} Entire Apartment with 2 accommodates generates the highest total stays (172,996 stays).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Question 4: Multi-listing Host Distribution}
\textbf{Question:} For hosts with multiple listings, are their properties concentrated within the same LGA or distributed across different LGAs?

\textbf{Analysis:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Distribution Pattern:} Overwhelming concentration strategy: 99.5\% of hosts (16,931) keep listings in the same LGA, while only 0.5\% (92) distribute across multiple LGAs.
\end{itemize}

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.7\textwidth]{images/q4_host_distribution_bar_proportional.png}
  \caption{Host distribution strategy showing concentrated vs distributed approaches.}
\end{figure}

\textbf{Key Finding:} Hosts strongly prefer geographic concentration, suggesting local market knowledge and operational efficiency drive this strategy.

\subsubsection{Question 5: Revenue vs Mortgage Coverage}
\textbf{Question:} For hosts with a single Airbnb listing, does the estimated revenue cover the annualised median mortgage repayment? Which LGA has the highest coverage percentage?

\textbf{Analysis:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Overall Coverage:} 56.1\% of single-listing hosts (7,939) can cover their mortgage, while 43.9\% (6,214) cannot.
\item \textbf{LGA Performance:} Bathurst Regional leads with 83.3\% coverage (5 out of 6 hosts), followed by Upper Hunter Shire (79.2\%) and Northern Beaches (76.3\%).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Summary}
The analysis reveals clear patterns: affluent, mature neighborhoods generate higher revenue; entire apartments optimize stays; hosts prefer geographic concentration; and most achieve mortgage coverage, particularly in regional areas.
